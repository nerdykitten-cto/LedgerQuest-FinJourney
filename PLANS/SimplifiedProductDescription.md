# FinJourney — Product Description
FinJourney [WORKING TITLE] is a personal finance management app with a campaign-driven RPG built inside it, targeting gamers, students, and casual users who struggle to maintain consistent financial habits.

On the finance side, users track expenses, manage budgets, photograph receipts, and set personal savings goals. On the game side, they act as an off-field commander watching a main character and party navigate a gripping, story-driven RPG campaign — completing quests, building relationships with party members, and uncovering a world with its own overarching narrative.

The two sides are directly linked through an action point system: daily habits and finance check-ins accumulate as action points throughout the day, and each evening the user watches those points play out in real time — their party advances through quests, completes objectives, and moves the story forward. Long-term financial goals, such as saving for a trip, a new device, or a restaurant outing with friends, are treated as major milestones that reward the player with something special: a rare item, a powerful weapon, or an entirely new party member joining the campaign.

The commitment loop mirrors Duolingo's daily pull, but the emotional weight of an unfolding RPG narrative — one the user cannot fast-track with money, only with real-life follow-through — is what makes them return.
